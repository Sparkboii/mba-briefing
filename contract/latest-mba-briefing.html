01 · Monetary policy · 5 Aug
RBI holds the repo rate at 5.25% and retains a neutral stance
RBI kept rates unchanged, with the reported FY27 growth forecast raised to 6.7% and inflation projection lowered to 5.0%, while retaining food, fuel, weather and geopolitical risks as watchpoints.
IMPLICATIONS ACROSS THE BUSINESS
Finance & strategy: steadier borrowing-cost assumptions improve planning and valuation visibility, but investment cases still need downside scenarios.Consumer & operations: stable rates can support credit-linked consumption; fuel and food shocks can still compress demand and margins.

02 · Banking & FX · 14 Aug
RBI closes its concessional FX-swap window early
Reuters reported that strong NRI deposit inflows—over $50 billion—allowed the central bank to end the discounted overseas-deposit FX-swap facility a month earlier than planned.
IMPLICATIONS ACROSS THE BUSINESS
Banks & treasury: funding availability may improve, but institutions must assess rollover durability and hedge costs.Corporates: FX liquidity and rupee conditions influence imports, overseas borrowing, pricing and capital-allocation choices.

04 · IT services · 20 Aug
AI pushes Indian IT contracts from billable hours toward outcomes
Reuters reported that clients are asking for productivity gains and lower prices, prompting more outcome-linked contracts. TCS said around 80% of certain business-services contracts are now outcome based.
IMPLICATIONS ACROSS THE BUSINESS
Revenue model: vendors take more delivery risk and need clear KPI baselines, pricing discipline and governance.Talent & delivery: AI changes staffing mixes, process design and the proof required to show client value.

IMPLICATIONS ACROSS THE BUSINESS
Portfolio & brand: reformulation, label design and trust will matter most for high-sugar, high-salt and processed categories.Finance & compliance: firms may face packaging, reformulation and regulatory costs long before demand effects are clear.
